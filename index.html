How it works

Set it up once. Then start a run whenever you like.

Most people are set up in about fifteen minutes.

  1. Fill in your profile

    Your details, work authorization, salary expectations and resume, plus answers to the questions employers ask most.

  2. Start a run

    Pick a job site, enter job titles and a location, and choose how many applications to send. Chrome opens and Moonshift gets to work.

  3. See what was sent

    Every application is listed with the answers it used. Questions it could not answer are saved for you, so you answer once and the next run reuses it.

Questions

Good to know

Is Moonshift a browser extension?

No. Moonshift is a desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux. It opens Google Chrome on your computer and applies in that window, so you can watch it work.

Which job sites does it work with?

LinkedIn (Easy Apply), Dice, ZipRecruiter and company career pages on Greenhouse, plus Naukri and Foundit in India. Glassdoor and Workday are on the way.

Can using it affect my job site accounts?

It can. Each job site sets its own rules about automated tools, and some do not allow them. Moonshift works at a steady pace and limits LinkedIn to 40 applications a day, but we cannot control how a site treats your account. Please read the terms of the sites you use.

Does my computer need to stay on?

Yes. Applications are sent from your computer, so keep it awake and online while a run is going.

How much does it cost?

$12 a month, or $29 for three months. Early access is opening to small groups at a time. See pricing for details.

Where is my information stored?

Your profile, resume, saved answers and job site logins stay on your computer. Read our privacy policy for the full picture.
