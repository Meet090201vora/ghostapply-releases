Questions

Billing and licenses

How do I get a license?

Moonshift is in early access and we are bringing people on in small groups. Request access, and when your spot opens we will email your license key and how to get started.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for and does not renew after that.

Is there a limit on applications?

No monthly limit. Some job sites have a daily safety limit to protect your account, for example 40 applications a day on LinkedIn.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

One license works on one computer. If you need it on a second computer, contact us.

What if it does not work on my computer?

If Moonshift will not run on a supported computer, email us within 14 days of your first payment and we will fix it or refund you.

Are taxes included?

Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax or VAT may be added at checkout depending on where you live.
