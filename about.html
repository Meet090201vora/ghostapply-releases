About us

We build the ghost
that carries the bag.

GhostApply is a young company making one thing: a desktop app that applies to jobs from the computer you already sit at. The mark is a ghost holding a job bag. That is the whole pitch.

The company

Small on purpose.

Most “auto apply” products want your password in the cloud and a browser you never see. That is a support nightmare and a trust problem. We started from the opposite: headed Chrome, local profiles, a license that lives in AppData, Dice first.

We are not a recruiting agency. We do not place you. We do not scrape a thousand boards on day one. We make the night of Easy Apply shorter, then we earn the next board.

Early Access means you are buying a working Dice engine and a seat at the table while LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, and Talent.com are still marked coming soon in the app.
