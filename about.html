About

We make job hunting less of a grind.

Moonshift is a small, independent software company with one product: a desktop app that takes the repetitive work out of applying for jobs.

Our story

Why we built it

Applying for jobs today means filling in the same forms again and again. Most people send dozens of applications a week, and every one asks for the same details, the same resume and the same answers.

We built Moonshift to do that part for you, on your own computer, in a browser you can see. You decide where to apply. Moonshift does the typing.

The name and the logo come from how most people use it: start a run in the evening and wake up to a list of sent applications. The witch with her suitcase is on her way to something new.

What we believe

How we work

Your data, your computer

Your resume, profile and job site logins stay on your machine. We only keep what we need to run your license.

Honest about what works

A job site is marked as available only when it works reliably. Until then it says coming soon.

You in control

Moonshift never logs in for you behind your back. You watch it work and step in whenever you want.
