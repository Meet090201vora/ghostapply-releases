How it works

Your job applications, on autopilot.

Moonshift is a desktop app that searches a job site for the roles you want and applies to them one by one in Chrome, using your profile and resume. You can watch every step and step in whenever a site needs you.

A run, step by step

What happens when you press Start

  1. Chrome opens on your computer

    Moonshift uses its own Chrome window, so your everyday browser is left alone. The first time, you log in to the job site in that window. After that it remembers you.

  2. It finds matching jobs

    Moonshift searches for the job titles and location you entered and skips jobs you have already applied to.

  3. It fills in each application

    Contact details, work authorization, experience and screening questions are answered from your profile and saved answers. Your resume is attached.

  4. You help when a site asks

    If a site wants a login code or a captcha, Moonshift waits while you type it in, then carries on.

  5. You get a full record

    Each application is saved with its status and the answers that were sent, with a link back to the job.

Inside the app

Everything in one place

Apply

Pick a job site, job titles, location and how many to send. Results appear as each application goes out.

Profile

Your details, work authorization, salary expectations, resume and your own saved answers. Edit them any time.

Answers

Every job Moonshift applied to, with its status, the answers it gave and a link to the listing.

Resume

A score for how well your resume reads to hiring software, with suggested fixes you approve line by line.

Gaps

Questions Moonshift could not answer. Answer each one once, save it to your profile, and future runs use it.

Settings

Your job site logins, your license and the app version. Moonshift updates itself.
