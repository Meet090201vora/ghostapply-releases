Product

A headed browser.

A short run.

GhostApply is not a Chrome extension and not a staffed click-farm. It is a desktop window that starts an engine on your machine and applies on Dice, ZipRecruiter and Greenhouse in Chrome you can see.

Boards

What you can run today

DiceLive

Easy Apply, resume attach, form fill with Gemini or a basic filler.

ZipRecruiterLive

1-click and quick apply from search results, with screening questions answered from your profile.

GreenhouseLive

Company career pages on Greenhouse — dropdowns, location pickers and optional questions handled.

Inside the app

Six rooms. Nothing extra.

Apply

Board, profile, keywords, location, max applications. Start. Watch Chrome, with live results as they land.

Profile

Applicant details, work authorization, compensation, resume and your own saved answers — all editable by you.

Answers

Every job the engine touched, with status, the answers it gave, and a link back.

Resume

An ATS-style score for your resume, the fixes that matter, and a rewrite you review line by line.

Gaps

Questions Gemini couldn’t answer. Answer once, save it to your profile in one click; the next run reuses it.

Settings

Board logins and sessions, your Gemini key, and the app version — which updates itself.
