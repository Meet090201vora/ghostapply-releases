Privacy

Last updated September 14, 2026.

GhostApply is a desktop product. Applicant profiles, resumes, Dice login, and Gemini keys are stored on your computer (typically under your Windows local app data). We do not operate a cloud copy of your resume as part of the core apply loop.

This website may collect the name and email you type on Contact or Checkout so we can send a license key or answer you. We will not sell that list. When Stripe (or another processor) is connected, their privacy policy covers card data — we will not put card numbers on our own forms.

The Windows WebView and Chrome sessions used by the app keep ordinary browser cookies for the job boards you sign into. That is between you and Dice (or a future board). GhostApply is not those sites.

Questions: use the Contact page. We will update this notice when billing or hosting actually ships.
